About QPDF

A free PDF toolkit that does its work on your device, so private documents can stay private.

Why QPDF exists

Almost everyone handles PDFs that should not be passed around: tax slips, bank statements, contracts, medical forms, identity documents, client files. Yet the quickest way to compress or merge a PDF is often to upload it to a website you know nothing about.

QPDF offers the same everyday tools — compress, merge, split, convert, sign, redact and more — without the upload. The software runs inside your browser, your file is processed on your own computer or phone, and the result is saved straight back to your device.

Who it is for

QPDF is a general-purpose toolkit for anyone who works with PDFs. Its privacy-first design is especially useful for people who handle other people’s confidential documents — lawyers, paralegals and legal assistants, immigration consultants, accountants, notaries and small businesses — as well as for anyone preparing personal paperwork such as applications, tax records or student files.

How it is built

QPDF is built on BentoPDF, an open-source, client-side PDF toolkit, together with other open-source engines such as PDF.js, pdf-lib, qpdf, PDFium, CoherentPDF, PyMuPDF, Ghostscript and Tesseract. QPDF adds its own design, organization, offline support and privacy hardening, and hosts every engine itself rather than loading it from third-party networks. The complete source code is available under the GNU AGPL-3.0 — see open-source licences.

Read how QPDF keeps your files private for the technical details, including a simple test you can run with your Wi-Fi switched off.

Made in Vancouver

QPDF is developed by wealththeory.ca in Vancouver, British Columbia. It is free to use, with no account, no subscription and no advertising.

A note on the name

QPDF (qpdf.ca) is not affiliated with qpdf, the open-source PDF transformation library by Jay Berkenbilt and contributors, although QPDF uses a WebAssembly build of that library for some operations and is grateful to its authors. QPDF is also not affiliated with BentoPDF, Adobe, or any government.

Questions and feedback

QPDF is provided as-is. If you have feedback, you can reach the developer through wealththeory.ca.
