Compression settings

Compression level

Fine-tune the compression engine. Picking a compression level resets these, except Override content protection, Convert to grayscale, Preserve PDF/A, and Brotli.

Qpdf.ca looks for the best quality that fits and tells you if the target cannot be reached. Leave empty to use the level as is.

{{#if targetLimit}}

Preset slightly below {{targetLimit}} on purpose: many upload forms require files smaller than their limit, and some count 1 MB as 1,000,000 bytes rather than 1,048,576. Check the size of the downloaded file before you upload it.

{{/if}}
