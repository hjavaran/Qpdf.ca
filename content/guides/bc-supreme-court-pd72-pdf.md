## Does PD-72 apply to your application?

British Columbia Supreme Court Practice Direction **PD-72**, effective **March 31, 2026**, permits electronic application records for **applications scheduled for 30 minutes or less before an associate judge in chambers** under the Associate Judges Chambers Pilot Project. It does **not** govern every BC Supreme Court filing, every chambers application or every matter heard by a judge. PD-72 rescinded the earlier PD-68 pilot direction.

The [official five-page PD-72 and Appendix A](https://www.bccourts.ca/supreme_court/practice_and_procedure/practice_directions/civil/PD-72_Electronic_Application_Records.pdf) are the controlling reference. They modify the usual civil and family application-record requirements for this defined process. Before using this checklist, confirm the hearing qualifies and check the applicable **Supreme Court Civil Rules** or **Supreme Court Family Rules** and any case-specific direction.

## The essential PD-72 PDF checklist

| Item | PD-72 treatment | Practical check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Filing route | Electronic application records **must** be submitted through Court Services Online (CSO) | Use the proper CSO filing flow |
| One combined file | Appendix A requires one organized, bookmarked and hyperlinked PDF | Open a single combined record, not a collection of loose files |
| True copies | Every included document **must** be identical or a true copy of the original | Check scanned stamps, signatures and all pages |
| Searchability | All documents **must** be searchable; scanned documents **must** be OCR-processed | Search text inside affidavits and exhibits |
| File size | Combined record **must not exceed 50 MB** | Check the final exported file size |
| Cover page | The applicant **should** use Form 30.001 or F32.2 as applicable, with party contact information | Place it first in the record |
| Index | The applicant **should** include a numbered, hyperlinked index second | Click every index destination |
| Bookmarks | Documents and exhibits **should** have clearly named bookmarks; bookmark panel **should** open on launch | Test the opening view and bookmark tree |
| Page numbers | Pages **should** be sequentially numbered at the top centre; PDF page search **must** match printed numbering | Type a page number into the viewer and compare |
| Security | Settings restricting viewing, printing, saving, annotating or searching **must** be disabled | Do not supply a password-protected or restricted file |

The distinction between **must** and **should** comes from Appendix A's actual wording. PD-72 paragraph 6 nevertheless requires compliance with the formatting requirements as a whole and says the Registry **will reject** records that do not comply. Treat its *should* formatting directions seriously; they are not merely aesthetic suggestions. [Read Appendix A directly](https://www.bccourts.ca/supreme_court/practice_and_procedure/practice_directions/civil/PD-72_Electronic_Application_Records.pdf) before filing.

## Assemble the record in the right order

Begin with the required material under the relevant court rules, then apply PD-72's electronic arrangement. Appendix A describes the cover page as the **first item** and a numbered hyperlinked index as the **second item**. After that, organize notices, responses, affidavits, exhibits and any included authorities so a reader can follow the index without guessing.

Unlike some standard paper application-record restrictions, PD-72 expressly allows the inclusion of affidavits of service, authorities and certain other documents, as addressed in paragraphs 4–5. More importantly, if a party intends to rely on case law or other authorities at the hearing, the party **must include copies** in the electronic application record. Appendix A further says an electronic book of authorities, when necessary, should be bookmarked, searchable and sequentially paginated; external links to sources may be added, but do not replace copies required by paragraph 4.

Use [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf/) to combine prepared working copies and [Reorder PDF Pages](/reorder-pdf-pages/) if the sequence needs correction. Preserve the untouched originals, especially where a document carries a certificate-based digital signature. Merging and stamping may affect its validation.

## Make scans searchable, not merely legible

An image-only scan may be readable to a person but impossible to search. PD-72 Appendix A requires OCR when an original text-based document must be scanned, such as an affidavit requiring a handwritten signature. Test several distinctive words across the scanned pages, not just one word on the cover. OCR can misread faint stamps, handwriting, small print and skewed scans, so the image must remain legible as well.

The most reliable starting point is a searchable PDF exported directly from the document's source application. Use [OCR PDF](/ocr-pdf/) on copies of scanned documents where appropriate, and check the result. **PD-72 does not impose a universal 300 DPI scanning rule.** Choose resolution that delivers readable, searchable content within the size limit.

## Create bookmarks, clickable index links and the right opening view

A **bookmark** lives in the PDF reader's navigation sidebar. An **index hyperlink** is a clickable entry on a page that jumps to the corresponding document or exhibit. PD-72 calls for both: an index with links to bookmarked documents, and meaningful bookmark names such as `Affidavit of Lee Chen, filed 12 May 2026` or `Exhibit B: Bank records`.

[Edit PDF Bookmarks](/edit-pdf-bookmarks/) can help organize the bookmark tree. **Creating bookmarks is not the same as creating hyperlinks in a visible index**, nor does it necessarily configure the reader to show the bookmark panel on opening. These are separate checks. Use PDF software capable of setting internal links and initial view where your workflow requires it; verify the exported result in a standard PDF viewer. If QPDF's present interface cannot achieve a specific PD-72 setting, use another capable editor rather than claiming the requirement has been met.

## Match printed page numbers to PDF page search

PD-72 says pages **should** be numbered consecutively, normally at the **top centre**. The sequence may start on the cover or **after the index**, provided it matches the index. It then states that *page searching in the PDF must match page numbering*.

Consider a record containing a cover, a two-page index and 37 pages of evidence. If the first evidence page is visibly numbered `1`, a plain viewer may call it physical page 4. **PDF Page Labels** can bridge this mismatch so that typing `1` locates the intended evidentiary page. [Add Page Numbers](/add-page-numbers-to-pdf/) prints visible numbers; [Add Page Labels](/add-page-labels/) affects the viewer's navigation labels. Test both rather than assuming either automatically configures the other.

If a file already has page numbers, do not stamp a contradictory sequence over them. Align the record's pagination, index references and viewer labels deliberately.

## Reduce file size without destroying the record

The combined file **must not exceed 50 MB**. Use [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf/) on an appropriate working copy when needed, then review photographs, handwriting, stamps and small print at full resolution. Compression targets are not guarantees; final file size depends on the source. Because the direction requires **one** application record, do not automatically split an oversized submission into unrelated files and assume it remains compliant. Rework the file or ask the Registry for guidance if necessary.

Before submitting, confirm that no password, permissions setting or encryption obstructs the Court's ability to open, print, save, annotate or search. Avoid [Sanitize PDF](/sanitize-pdf/) on a finished linked record unless you specifically intend to remove links, attachments or accessibility structure and will rebuild and recheck anything required.

## Submission timing and special situations

PD-72 requires filing through **Court Services Online** and encourages early submission because a rejected file may be resubmitted only within the applicable court-rule timelines. Check the relevant civil or family rule for the specific filing deadline and any directions in your matter; this guide does not substitute a universal cutoff for those rules.

A paper copy of the application record is **not required** when a compliant PD-72 electronic record is submitted. If a matter is adjourned to a specific date, the electronic record remains available for that next hearing; if adjourned **generally**, it must be resubmitted when the matter is reset. PD-72 paragraph 11 also sets out a special vetted-draft-order process for parties authorized to attend chambers remotely and seeking an order signed on the bench; the copy included in the record **must** be scanned and OCR-processed.

## Five-minute pre-submission audit

- [ ] The hearing is an eligible **30-minute-or-less associate-judge chambers application**.
- [ ] The PDF is one combined, organized record under **50 MB**.
- [ ] Cover and index appear first and second as described; all included documents are true copies.
- [ ] Every scan has been OCR-processed and a sample of each document is searchable.
- [ ] Bookmark titles are meaningful; the bookmark panel's opening state has been checked.
- [ ] Each index entry jumps to the correct document or exhibit.
- [ ] Printed numbers, index references and PDF page labels agree.
- [ ] Authorities needed at the hearing are included, not simply linked externally.
- [ ] The file is not password protected or restricted, and opens in a different PDF viewer.
- [ ] Filing deadline and CSO submission confirmation have been checked separately from PDF formatting.
