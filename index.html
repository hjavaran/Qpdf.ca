How local processing works

Qpdf.ca sends your browser the software, not the other way around. The PDF engines run inside the page using JavaScript and WebAssembly, so your document never needs to travel to a server.

Choose a file

Your browser reads the PDF from your device into memory. Nothing is sent over the network.

Process in your browser

The tool works on your device’s processor. Supported tools keep working even with Wi-Fi switched off.

Save the result

The finished PDF is created in memory and saved straight to your device. Close the tab and it is gone.
